**Amazon's FY2017 DPO ≈ 93.86 days**

Using the line items from the Consolidated Balance Sheets and Consolidated Statements of Operations in Amazon's FY2017 10-K (in $ millions):

**Inputs (from the financial statements):**

| Line item | FY2016 | FY2017 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounts payable | $25,309 | $34,616 | Consolidated Balance Sheets |
| Inventories | $11,461 | $16,047 | Consolidated Balance Sheets |
| Cost of sales (COGS) | — | $111,934 | Consolidated Statements of Operations |

**Calculation:**

1. **Average accounts payable** = (25,309 + 34,616) / 2 = **$29,962.5M**
2. **Change in inventory** = 16,047 − 11,461 = **$4,586M**
3. **Adjusted purchases denominator** = FY2017 COGS + Δ inventory = 111,934 + 4,586 = **$116,520M**
4. **DPO** = 365 × 29,962.5 / 116,520 = 93.8578…

**DPO ≈ 93.86 days** — meaning Amazon took roughly 94 days on average to pay its suppliers during FY2017, consistent with its well-known strategy of using supplier financing (negative working capital cycle) to fund operations.
