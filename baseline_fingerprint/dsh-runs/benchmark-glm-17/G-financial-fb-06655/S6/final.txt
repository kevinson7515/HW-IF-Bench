## Answer: Amazon's FY2017 DPO ≈ **93.86 days**

All figures are taken directly from the attached FY2017 10-K (in $ millions):

**From the Consolidated Balance Sheets (Dec 31, 2016 and 2017):**
| Line item | FY2016 | FY2017 |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts payable | $25,309 | $34,616 |
| Inventories | $11,461 | $16,047 |

**From the Consolidated Statements of Operations (Year Ended Dec 31, 2017):**
- Cost of sales (FY2017): $111,934

**Calculation:**
1. Average accounts payable = (25,309 + 34,616) / 2 = **$29,962.5**
2. Change in inventory = 16,047 − 11,461 = **$4,586**
3. Adjusted purchases denominator = COGS + Δinventory = 111,934 + 4,586 = **$116,520**
4. DPO = 365 × 29,962.5 / 116,520 = **93.86 days** (93.8578 unrounded)

This indicates Amazon took roughly 94 days on average to pay its suppliers in FY2017 — a notably long payables cycle that reflects Amazon's negotiating leverage with vendors, effectively using supplier financing to fund its operations.
