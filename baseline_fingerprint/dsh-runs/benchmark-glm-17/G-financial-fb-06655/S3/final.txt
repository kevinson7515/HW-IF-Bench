## Answer: Amazon's FY2017 DPO ≈ **93.86 days**

### Inputs from the attached 10-K (in $ millions)

**Consolidated Balance Sheets** (December 31, 2016 / 2017):
| Line item | 2016 | 2017 |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts payable | 25,309 | 34,616 |
| Inventories | 11,461 | 16,047 |

**Consolidated Statements of Operations** (Year Ended December 31, 2017):
| Line item | 2017 |
|---|---|
| Cost of sales | 111,934 |

### Calculation
1. **Average accounts payable** = (25,309 + 34,616) ÷ 2 = **29,962.5**
2. **Change in inventory** = 16,047 − 11,461 = **4,586**
3. **Adjusted COGS** = 111,934 + 4,586 = **116,520**
4. **DPO** = 365 × 29,962.5 ÷ 116,520 = 10,936,312.5 ÷ 116,520 ≈ **93.86**

So, using the line items from the balance sheet and P&L statement in the attached filing, Amazon's FY2017 days payable outstanding is **93.86 days** (rounded to two decimal places).
