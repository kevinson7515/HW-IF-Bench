## Method

The custody records independently corroborate that hierarchy: CB-DOC-001-073…084 state, for every key, that the operative record was "fixed at" the operative-authority (R-reference) record while the current-operations extract (E-reference) is only a later-collected copy — so R473 (Talia Brooks) controls the consent requirement and R293 (Owen Delgado) controls the anti-assignment approval over the conflicting extracts. Where a record's reliance limits preclude a conclusion — the CBP-001-01 entity collision and the inactive CBP-001-11 roster — the item is routed to the signing path for human resolution rather than guessed. No legal effect beyond the source records is asserted.
