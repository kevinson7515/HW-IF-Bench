Amazon's FY2017 days payable outstanding (DPO) is **93.86 days**.

Using the line items from the FY2017 10-K balance sheet and statement of operations (all figures in $ millions):

**Balance sheet:**
- Accounts payable: $25,309 (Dec 31, 2016) and $34,616 (Dec 31, 2017)
- Inventories: $11,461 (Dec 31, 2016) and $16,047 (Dec 31, 2017)

**P&L statement:**
- Cost of sales (COGS): $111,934 (FY2017)

**Calculation:**

- Average accounts payable = (25,309 + 34,616) / 2 = **29,962.5**
- Change in inventory = 16,047 − 11,461 = **4,586**
- COGS + change in inventory = 111,934 + 4,586 = **116,520**

DPO = 365 × 29,962.5 / 116,520 = 93.8578… ≈ **93.86**
