Amazon's FY2017 **days payable outstanding (DPO) is 93.86 days**.

Here are the line items pulled from the 10-K (all figures in $ millions):

**Balance sheet**
- Accounts payable: $25,309 (2016) → $34,616 (2017)
- Inventories: $11,461 (2016) → $16,047 (2017)

**Income statement**
- Cost of sales (COGS): $111,934 (2017)

**Calculation**
- Average accounts payable = (25,309 + 34,616) / 2 = **29,962.5**
- Change in inventory = 16,047 − 11,461 = **4,586**
- Denominator = COGS + change in inventory = 111,934 + 4,586 = **116,520**
- DPO = 365 × 29,962.5 / 116,520 = **93.8578…**

Rounded to two decimal places: **93.86 days**.
