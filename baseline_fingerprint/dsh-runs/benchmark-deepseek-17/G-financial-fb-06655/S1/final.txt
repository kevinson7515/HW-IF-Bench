Amazon's FY2017 days payable outstanding (DPO) is **93.86 days**.

Here's the calculation using the line items from the 10-K:

**From the Consolidated Balance Sheets (page 40):**
- Accounts payable: FY2016 = $25,309M, FY2017 = $34,616M
- Inventories: FY2016 = $11,461M, FY2017 = $16,047M

**From the Consolidated Statements of Operations (page 38):**
- Cost of sales (COGS): FY2017 = $111,934M

**Calculation:**

1. Average accounts payable = (25,309 + 34,616) / 2 = **$29,962.5M**
2. Change in inventory = 16,047 − 11,461 = **$4,586M**
3. Denominator = 111,934 + 4,586 = **$116,520M**
4. DPO = 365 × 29,962.5 / 116,520 = **93.86 days** (rounded to two decimal places)
