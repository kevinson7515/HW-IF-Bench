# 3M FY2018 Capital Expenditure

**Answer: $1,577 million**

## Source

The amount is taken from 3M's FY2018 Form 10-K, **Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows**, under *Cash Flows from Investing Activities*.

The line item is:

> **Purchases of property, plant and equipment (PP&E)** — 2018: **(1,577)**

This line represents 3M's capital expenditures for fiscal year 2018.
