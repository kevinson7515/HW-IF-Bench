# 3M FY2018 Capital Expenditure

## Question
What is the FY2018 capital expenditure amount (in USD millions) for 3M, based on the cash flow statement?

## Answer
**$1,577 million**

## Source
3M Company and Subsidiaries — 2018 Form 10-K, **Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows** (page 60).

The line item under *Cash Flows from Investing Activities* reads:

| (Millions) | 2018 | 2017 | 2016 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purchases of property, plant and equipment (PP&E) | (1,577) | (1,373) | (1,420) |

The fiscal year 2018 column shows **purchases of property, plant and equipment (PP&E) of $1,577 million** (shown in parentheses as a cash outflow). This is the capital expenditure amount for FY2018.

The same figure appears in the Management's Discussion and Analysis "Cash Flows from Investing Activities" section (page 46).
