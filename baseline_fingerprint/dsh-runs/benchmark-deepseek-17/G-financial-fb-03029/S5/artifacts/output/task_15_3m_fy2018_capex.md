# 3M FY2018 Capital Expenditure

**Question:** What is the FY2018 capital expenditure amount (in USD millions) for 3M?

**Answer:** **$1,577 million** (USD millions)

## Source

From the 3M 2018 Form 10-K, **Consolidated Statement of Cash Flow** (page 60), under *Cash Flows from Investing Activities*:

| Line item | 2018 |
| --- | --- |
| Purchases of property, plant and equipment (PP&E) | $(1,577) |

Capital expenditure is reported on the cash flow statement as "Purchases of property, plant and equipment (PP&E)." For the year ended December 31, 2018, this amount is **$1,577 million**.
