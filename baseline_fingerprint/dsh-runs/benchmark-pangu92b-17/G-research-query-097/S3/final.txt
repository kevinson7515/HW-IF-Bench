**Verdict: plausible but conditional — measurable, "significant" depends on context.**

| For | Against |
|---|---|
| RAA: 570 US small-community airports → $134B activity, 1M jobs | EU auditors: €666m wasted; 7/20 EU-funded airports unprofitable |
| ACRP 218: non-hub = 0.0114 jobs/departing seat | Causality unclear; multipliers overstate; small airports ≪ hubs |
| Blonigen–Cristea: +50% passengers → +3.2% income, +5.5% jobs | Forecasts wrong (3M predicted vs 1.1M actual) |

500k pax ≈ 250k enplanements × 0.0114 ≈ ~2,850 associated jobs (+ direct jobs, visitor spend).

Bottom line: measurable yes; "significant" only for a small region; hinges on connectivity, catchment demand, sustained service.
