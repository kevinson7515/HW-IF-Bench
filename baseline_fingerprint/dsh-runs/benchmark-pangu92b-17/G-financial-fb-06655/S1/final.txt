## Amazon FY2017 DPO: **93.86**

**Inputs from the 10-K (in millions):**

| Line item | FY2016 | FY2017 |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts payable | $25,309 | $34,616 |
| Inventories | $11,461 | $16,047 |
| Cost of sales (COGS) | — | $111,934 |

**Calculation:**

- Average accounts payable = (25,309 + 34,616) / 2 = **29,962.5**
- Change in inventory = 16,047 − 11,461 = **4,586**
- Denominator = 111,934 + 4,586 = **116,520**

DPO = 365 × 29,962.5 / 116,520 = 93.8578… → **93.86 days**
