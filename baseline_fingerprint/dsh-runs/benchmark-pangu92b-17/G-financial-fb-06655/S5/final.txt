## Amazon FY2017 DPO = **93.86**

### Line items used (from the 10-K)

| Item | FY2016 | FY2017 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounts payable | $25,309M | $34,616M | Consolidated Balance Sheets |
| Inventories | $11,461M | $16,047M | Consolidated Balance Sheets |
| Cost of sales (FY2017) | — | $111,934M | Consolidated Statements of Operations |

### Calculation

- Average accounts payable = (25,309 + 34,616) / 2 = **29,962.5**
- Change in inventory = 16,047 − 11,461 = **4,586**
- Denominator = 111,934 + 4,586 = **116,520**
- DPO = 365 × 29,962.5 / 116,520 = **93.8578…** → **93.86**
