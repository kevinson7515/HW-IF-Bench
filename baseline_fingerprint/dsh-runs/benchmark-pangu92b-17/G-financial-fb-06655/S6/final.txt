**Amazon FY2017 DPO = 93.86 days**

Using the line items from the 10-K:

| Item | FY2016 | FY2017 |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts payable (balance sheet) | $25,309M | $34,616M |
| Inventories (balance sheet) | $11,461M | $16,047M |
| Cost of sales (P&L) | — | $111,934M |

Calculation:

- Average accounts payable = (25,309 + 34,616) / 2 = **29,962.5**
- Change in inventory = 16,047 − 11,461 = **4,586**
- Denominator = 111,934 + 4,586 = **116,520**
- DPO = 365 × 29,962.5 / 116,520 = 93.8578…

**Rounded to two decimal places: 93.86 days**
