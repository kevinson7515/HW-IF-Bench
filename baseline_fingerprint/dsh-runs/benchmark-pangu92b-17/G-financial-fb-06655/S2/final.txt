**Amazon FY2017 DPO = 93.86**

Using the 10-K line items (in millions):

- Accounts payable: FY2016 = $25,309; FY2017 = $34,616 → average = $29,962.5
- Inventories: FY2016 = $11,461; FY2017 = $16,047 → Δ inventory = $4,586
- FY2017 Cost of sales = $111,934

DPO = 365 × 29,962.5 / (111,934 + 4,586) = 365 × 29,962.5 / 116,520 = **93.86**
