## Answer

**Gross margin is not a relevant metric for JPMorgan Chase & Co.**, so the "consistency" question doesn't apply. I can't compute it, and no such figure is reported.

**Why:** JPMorgan is a financial holding company (bank), not a manufacturer or retailer. It has no "cost of goods sold." A bank's "cost of revenue" is mainly interest paid on deposits and wholesale funding, which is already netted into **net interest income** — not a COGS line. Its income statement instead runs: Total net revenue → noninterest expense → provision for credit losses → income before tax. The metrics that matter for a bank are net interest margin, the efficiency/overhead ratio, ROTCE, and ROE — not gross margin.
