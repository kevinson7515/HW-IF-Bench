### 6. Rationale for the two flagged gaps

- **Customer-consent (CBP-001-11).** The largest automaker customer can terminate on an indirect change of control. A termination right cannot be priced away; it must be eliminated or waived before the transfer completes. The operative authority (Talia Brooks, R473) fixes who must give the consent. This is therefore a **closing condition**, not a priced exception.

- **Tooling (CBP-001-10).** The Nevada cathode-tooling exposure is the sales-tax nexus gap. The operative authority limits the tax authority to **Washoe County, Nevada** (R710) — a geographically bounded exposure that can be quantified, indemnified, or escrowed. It does not "block completion." This is therefore a **priced exception**, not a closing condition.
