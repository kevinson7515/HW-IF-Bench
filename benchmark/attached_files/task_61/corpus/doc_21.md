Student Scholars creates pipelines to careers in world affairs and fosters a culture of lifelong learning.

Established in 2010 with generous support from the Malú and Carlos Alvarez Family, the Student Scholars Program offers 25 undergraduate students from Council communities the opportunity to attend the National Conference at no cost. WACA also supports the travel, room, and board of program participants.

While in Washington, the students also attend Scholar Day, an exclusive excursion into DC that includes visits to think tanks, embassies, international organizations, and more. Through these experiences, students map their own global futures and prepare to take important next steps in their education and careers.

Links to the nomination forms for both Councils and students are below. Also, to help Councils recruit Student Scholars, WACA has created a media kit.

WACA provides funding to the nominating Council in the amount of $1,250 to help defray travel and lodging (double occupancy) for their Student Scholar. Councils arrange transportation and hotel reservations that must include attendance on all three days of the National Conference. To help Councils find roommates for their students, WACA shares the list of accepted Student Scholars with all sponsoring Councils.

Eligibility

A Council may nominate one undergraduate student, 18 years or older, from within their local communities.

To broaden the reach of the WACA network, we appreciate local Councils promoting Student Scholars widely among local institutions of higher education. Among equally-qualified applicants of promise, preference is given to nominees who may not yet have significant local Council involvement.

The following students are not eligible to apply:

- High school students

- Graduate students

- College students in Greater Washington, DC ("the DMV")

- Applicants from prior years

Nomination Process

The Council must submit a(n):

- Online nomination form (link above)

- Letter of recommendation from professor of nominee

The student must submit a(n):

- Online application form (link above)

- Current resume

- 1-2 page Letter of Intent

One student per Council may be nominated. Multiple student nominees from the same Council will not be considered.

Application Deadline: Friday, September 26, 2025, 5 PM EDT.

Nominated students will be reviewed by the national office in consultation with the WACA National Board of Directors. Selections will be announced within 2 weeks after the application deadline.

As part of their participation requirement, Student Scholars submit a brief evaluation and report on their experience no later than two weeks after National Conference.

For further questions, please email Dr. Dan Villanueva, WACA Global Education Manager.
